Alchemy 1-300

World of Warcraft: Forever, Horde. The route, what to buy, and where to train.

Biggest change from Classic: healing potions moved to First Aid, so old Classic routes no longer work.

Pair it with Herbalism, which supplies most of the herbs. You'll still need to buy vials and get the fish.

Recipes you have to buy

Superior Mana Potion

Algernon, Undercity. Needed at skill 265.

Major Frenzy Potion

Apothecary Durelle, at the Durotar camp outside the Crossroads, for Merchant's Favor. Needed at skill 290.

Draught of Detect Undead

The one gap. Guides name Master Mycologist Shurome in Felwood, but Horde access isn't confirmed. Check before stocking up on Arthas' Tears.

New in Forever

Gold makers

  • Transmutes are named as steady income. Iron to Gold and Mithril to Truesilver come at 225, with more at 275.
  • Flasks are used up, so they're named as a repeat market.
  • Guides also name Greater Fire Protection and Free Action Potions.

Camp and bonuses

  • Mana Well restores mana to nearby players. Guides give skill 20 or 5.
  • Fermenter at 140 and Alchemy Laboratory at 300 both need blueprints.
  • Mixology, a stronger and longer elixir bonus, is unconfirmed.

Worth checking for your classes

  • Shaman: Strength elixirs from 150, Greater Strength at 245, Brute Force at 275.
  • Hunter: Minor Agility at 50, up to Greater Agility at 240.
  • Both: Mongoose at 280.

My picks from the names. No guide covers these classes, so check the tooltips.

What isn't confirmed

All of this is pre-launch data, and the beta stopped short of 300, so everything past about 225 is planned rather than tested.

  • Fire Oil gives 2 per craft in most guides and 3 in one, so you may need fewer crafts.
  • Guides also differ on the route from 230 to 290. Metabot uses a different recipe line.
  • The Gfinity guide still uses healing potions, so ignore its route.
