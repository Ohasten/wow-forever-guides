WoW Forever Macros

Copy a macro, paste it in the game, and drag it to your action bar.

  1. Click Copy on a macro.
  2. In the game, type /macro and click New.
  3. Name it, pick an icon, and paste with Ctrl+V.
  4. Drag it to your action bar.
