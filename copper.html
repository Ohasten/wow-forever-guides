Sell copper bars or craft armor?

World of Warcraft Classic, Blacksmithing up to skill 35

Selling to a vendor: make Copper Battle Axes if you have 2 Malachite, for about +2s 23c each. Without Malachite, make Copper Chain Pants for +27c each.

Selling on the auction house: plain bars are likely better. Sell bars instead of vendoring axes if a bar earns over about 29c after the auction house cut, or instead of vendoring pants if it earns over about 17c.

Ingredients are valued at what a vendor would pay for them: Copper Bar 10c, Rough Stone 2c, Rough Grinding Stone 5c, Linen Cloth 13c, Light Leather 15c, Malachite 15c. Weak Flux is valued at its 1s purchase price. Heavy Copper Longsword is left out: it's Alliance-only, and its listed 27c price looks wrong next to the other weapons.

Good to know

  • Smelting: always smelt before selling to a vendor. Ore sells for 5c and a bar for 10c. You need the Mining profession to smelt.
  • Auction house: one Classic guide puts bars at about 0.75–1g per stack, or roughly 4–5s per bar with 20 in a stack. That guide may be out of date. Only craft items to sell there if they go for more than their ingredients do, after the auction house cut.
  • Malachite: it only drops from about 1% of Copper Veins. If you have to buy it, the axe stops being worth it above about 1s 26c per Malachite. Check whether yours is worth more on the auction house before using it.
  • Chain Vest: the plans are a world drop, and how easy they are to get is unconfirmed. It's only worth considering if you already have them.
  • Skill points: the pants should give a guaranteed skill point on every craft up to 50, and the Battle Axe up to 75. You may need to learn these recipes from a trainer first, and training costs aren't included here.

Before you craft a big batch

  • These are Classic wiki prices. If you play a different version, check them in-game.
  • Live auction prices for your realm aren't included, so compare bars and pants there first.
