Orc Shaman Weapons

World of Warcraft: Forever, Horde, levels 8 to 60. Slow two-handers ranked by the biggest single hit, not DPS.

How this was checked. Damage, speed and quest IDs come from Wowhead's Forever database on 5 November 2026. Drop chances and some locations come from pre-launch guides, and nothing was tested in the live game.

The route: biggest hit at each level

    "Hit" is the weapon's top damage roll before your attack power is added. Many of these are world drops, so check the Auction House. If one is too expensive, use the next best weapon in the list below.

    Before you start

    Why slow wins here

    • Every hit is a bigger number, so a swing can take a big chunk of a mob's health.
    • Pick the higher top hit first, then the slower speed if two are close.

    Weapon skills for Orc

    • You start with maces, staves and shields, so staves work straight away.
    • If a two-hander shows red, train it.
    • Axes: Sayoc or Hanashi, Orgrimmar
    • Maces: Ansekhwa, Thunder Bluff

    Weapon imbues

    • Level 1: Rockbiter Weapon
    • Level 30: Windfury Weapon, which is a big power jump with a two-hander
