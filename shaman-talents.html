Enhancement Talent Tree

World of Warcraft: Forever. The shaman talent tree, filled in level by level for a slow two-hander.

How this was checked. The tree layout and requirements come from Wowhead's Forever calculator on 5 November 2026, and the leveling order meets every requirement shown there. The order comes from Leprestore. Most guides were written during the level 30 beta, so check the in-game tree before spending points.

The tree

Stat priority with Mental Dexterity

Priority once you have it

  1. Weapon damage: a slow two-hander first
  2. Hit up to 8% for raid bosses
  3. Crit
  4. Intellect and Strength: about equal
  5. Stamina

Why Intellect climbs

  • Mental Dexterity turns Intellect into Attack Power: 100% at rank 1, and Maxroll gives 1 to 1.5 for the full talent.
  • Ancestral Knowledge adds 10% Intellect at rank 1.
  • Mental Quickness adds spell damage from Intellect too.
  • Intellect also gives mana, your main cause of downtime.

Where guides differ

  • wow.gg puts Strength above Intellect.
  • A Forever forum post puts Intellect a bit above Strength once the talents are in.
  • Mobalytics says Intellect and Spirit matter most while leveling.
  • No guide has tested numbers, so treat Intellect and Strength as close.
