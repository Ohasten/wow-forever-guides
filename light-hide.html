Getting Light Hide in WoW Forever

Research report for a Horde player, 5 November 2026. What's still vanilla, what changed, and the quickest way to get it.

Fastest: buy it on the Auction House, if the price is fair. Skinning a full batch of 27 takes hours.

Easiest to farm: Foothills Duskbats in northern Silverpine Forest, a mob new to Forever with the best listed rate. Mottled Worgs sit in the same area as a proven backup.

Which way to go

Buy it if

  • You need it now, or only need a few.
  • The price beats what your time earns. Compare the time above with your gold per hour.
  • You don't have Skinning.

Farm it if

  • You're level 12 or higher and questing nearby anyway.
  • You also want Light Leather, which you'll get far more of.
  • You're leveling Skinning, so the kills pay twice.

How to farm it

  1. Train Skinning from Thuwd in Orgrimmar, Kagil in Durotar or Killian Hagey in Undercity, and carry a Skinning Knife in your bags.
  2. Start with Duskbats around 53, 16 in Silverpine.
  3. Track your hides for 50 skins. Under 2 or 3 means the rate is low, so move to Mottled Worgs or the Barrens.
  4. Keep every Light Leather to sell or use.

What changed from vanilla

Still the same

  • Light Hide comes from skinning beasts around levels 9 to 25, as a rare extra next to Light Leather.
  • Wowhead flags nearly all its skinnable mobs as unchanged.
  • Core Skinning is largely as it was, per Warcraft Tavern.
  • It's a salt-cured reagent: Light Hide plus Salt makes Cured Light Hide.

New in Forever

  • New skinnable mobs: Foothills Duskbat in Silverpine and Gray Fox in Duskwood.
  • Ressan the Needler in Tirisfal is marked as updated.
  • Skill gear: Keen Skinning Knife +3, Cartilage Shapers +4, and glove enchants +5 and +10.
  • Waylaid Crates give Cured Light Hide a new use, taking 2 per Apprentice Fabrics crate.

Doesn't help here

  • Bountiful Harvest boosts Scarce materials, and nothing says Light Hide counts.
  • Mob drops without skinning are tiny, mostly under 1%. Wolfguard Worgs in Shadowfang Keep show about 12%, but from only 118 samples.
  • Chests give it only about 2 to 5% of the time.
  • No vendor sells it, as far as I could find.

How much you actually need

The wow-professions Leatherworking route uses 27 Light Hide at most: cure them all, then keep 24 for Light Leather Pants at skill 100. Each one needs a Salt from a Leatherworking Supplies vendor. Those are the optional routes, so you may need fewer. Hides also sell to vendors for only 50 copper, so don't overfarm.

What isn't confirmed

Treat every rate as a guide, not a promise.

  • The Duskbat rate is thin: 11.1% comes from just 45 skins. The true rate could be roughly 5% to 24%.
  • Other rates may be older: Wowhead's large samples, like Mottled Worg's 23,943, probably include Classic data. Wowhead marks those mobs unchanged, but nobody has tested them on live Forever yet.
  • Skinning rule conflict: one guide says 5 times the mob's level, another says lower for mobs under 20. Your tooltip will show which is right.
  • No guide covers this: no Forever guide names a Light Hide farm or a price. The ranking here is my own, built from Wowhead's numbers.
  • Prices: check your own Auction House. Forever launched yesterday, so prices will move a lot.
