Orc Hunter Weapons

World of Warcraft: Forever, Horde, levels 3 to 60. Slow bows, guns and crossbows ranked by the biggest single shot, not DPS.

How this was checked. Damage, speed and quest IDs come from Wowhead's Forever database on 5 November 2026. Drop chances and vendor locations come from pre-launch guides and Classic data, and nothing was tested in the live game.

The route: biggest shot at each level

    "Shot" is the weapon's top damage roll, before ammo, attack power and abilities. Route picks favour weapons you can reliably get. World drops can hit harder, so check the Auction House as you level.

    Before you start

    Why slow wins here

    • One Marksmanship guide recommends slow weapons because Aimed Shot damage is based on weapon damage.
    • Aimed Shot is baseline at level 20 and shares a cooldown with Multi-Shot.
    • Keep your arrows or bullets upgraded, plus your quiver or ammo pouch.

    Weapon skills for Orc

    • You start with bows, axes and daggers, so bows work straight away.
    • Guns: Ansekhwa, Thunder Bluff
    • Crossbows: Archibald, Undercity
    • Hunters can't use maces, so most Shaman picks don't carry over.
