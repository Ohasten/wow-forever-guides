Orc Hunter Weapons

World of Warcraft: Forever, Horde, levels 3 to 60. Ranged weapons ranked by the biggest single shot, with DPS shown beside it.

How this was checked. Damage, speed and quest IDs come from Wowhead's Forever database on 5 November 2026. Drop chances and vendor locations come from pre-launch guides and Classic data, and nothing was tested in the live game.

The route: biggest shot at each level

    "Shot" is the weapon's top damage roll, before ammo, attack power and abilities. Route picks favour weapons you can reliably get, so world drops can beat them.

    Before you start

    Slow or high DPS?

    • Slow: wow.gg says Aimed Shot and Sniper Shot use weapon damage, so it prefers a slow weapon.
    • High DPS: Maxroll and ForeverChanges say to always take the highest DPS.
    • Auto Shot was about a third of the damage in one Marksmanship sim, so DPS still counts.
    • Pick the slower weapon when DPS is close, not when it's far behind.

    Weapon skills for Orc

    • You start with bows, axes and daggers, so bows work straight away.
    • Guns: Ansekhwa, Thunder Bluff
    • Crossbows and polearms: Archibald, Undercity. Polearms need level 20.
    • Hunters can't use maces, so most Shaman picks don't carry over.

    Stats, ammo and melee

    • Agility first. It gives 2 ranged attack power per point.
    • Buy better ammo at levels 10, 25 and 40, and keep a quiver or ammo pouch.
    • Pets don't depend on your weapon, so pet choice doesn't change this list.
